# Nivalis Staff Order

Put your venue's staff in the order you like. Each person in the venue's **Staff** tab gets up and down arrows (next to the fire button): click them to move that person up or down the list, so your cooks sit together and your manager is on top.

Display only: the order doesn't change who works when or what they do first. Shifts, roles and the game's task priority still decide that. The order is kept with your save, per venue, and new hires join at the bottom.

## Installation

Needs BepInEx 6 (IL2CPP, be.788) and [Nivalis ModKit](https://github.com/BGASM/NivalisModKit) 0.2.0 or later. Extract the zip into the game folder: `BepInEx\plugins\NivalisStaffOrder.dll`.
